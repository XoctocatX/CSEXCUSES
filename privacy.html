Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

This privacy policy describes how customersupportexcuses.com ("we", "us", "the site") handles information when you visit the site or use any application hosted on its subdomains, including app.customersupportexcuses.com (the "Application").

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous analytics: aggregated visit data via Google Analytics (page views, browser type, country) — no personal identification.
  • Account data (Application only): email address, name, and authentication tokens you provide via Google OAuth when you sign in.
  • Google Business Profile data (Application only): when you authorize the Application, we access business listings you manage, including reviews, posts, photos, and performance metrics, solely to display and manage them on your behalf.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the Application's core features (managing your Google Business Profile listings).
  • To improve site reliability and user experience.
  • To respond to support requests.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers strictly necessary to operate the Application (hosting, analytics).
  • Google, when you authenticate or when the Application calls Google APIs on your behalf.
  • Law enforcement, when legally compelled.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

The Application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of account removal. Anonymous analytics data is retained per Google Analytics defaults.

6. Your Rights

You can revoke the Application's access to your Google account at any time via your Google account permissions page. To request data deletion, contact us at the address below.

7. Cookies

The site uses cookies set by Google Analytics for anonymous traffic measurement. No advertising or tracking cookies are set by us.

8. Children

The site and Application are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact
